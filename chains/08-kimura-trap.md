# The Kimura Trap Chain

**Half guard bottom → Kimura grip → Opponent defends → Counters**

Once the kimura grip is locked from bottom half guard, every common defense has a counter — including the one where they escape to north-south, which you can bait on purpose.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    HG["Bottom half guard<br/>opponent presents an arm"] --> KG["Kimura grip"]
    HG -->|"fake the kimura, swim under"| OCT["Octopus guard chain"]
    KG -->|"they grab their inner thigh"| FR["Work the grip free,<br/>flatten their back"] --> FIN["Finish the kimura"]
    KG -->|"they clasp hands<br/>and posture up"| DR["Drape your leg over<br/>their head / shoulder"] --> FIN
    DR -->|"they keep posturing"| AB["Pivot to armbar"]
    KG -->|"they free their legs,<br/>go north-south"| ROT["Rotate underneath"] --> BC["Over-rotate to the back"]
```
