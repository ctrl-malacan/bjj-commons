# The Front Headlock System

**Front headlock → Drag the arm across → Anaconda, D'Arce, head-and-arm, guillotine, or the back**

Everything from the front headlock runs through one question: does their arm come across their center line? If it does, you choose the finish. If they fight it, you threaten something else until it does. If they turtle, the [turtle attack chain](03-turtle-attack.md) takes over.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    IN["Wrestle-up sprawl · turtle ·<br/>scramble · two-on-one to whizzer"] --> FH["Front headlock<br/>gable grip, or two hands on the tricep"]
    FH -->|"they turtle"| T["Turtle attack chain"]
    FH -->|"pull the arm across"| Q{"Does the arm come across?"}
    Q -->|"they push out with the elbow"| PG["Provocation guillotine<br/>swim around the shoulder"]
    Q -->|"yes"| ACR["Arm across the center line"]
    ACR -->|"gable behind the tricep, roll through"| AN["Anaconda"]
    AN -->|"it fades"| NS["Tricep grip, get to the knees →<br/>north-south or side control"]
    ACR -->|"slip the arm under the armpit"| DA["D'Arce"]
    ACR -->|"switch to tricep + head"| HA["Head-and-arm choke"]
    ACR -->|"clasp the tricep, walk around"| BC["Back control"]
    Q -->|"no: they fight the drag"| SO["Step the left leg over the head"]
    SO -->|"no reaction"| PN["Right leg over the back →<br/>Peruvian necktie (low %)"]
    SO -->|"they push the leg"| KN["Right knee walks in,<br/>arm comes across, grip cinches"]
    KN -->|"they shove the leg off"| AG["Switch legs to their side, fall back →<br/>arm triangle guillotine"]
    AG -->|"they turn away"| FS["Right arm under the far shoulder,<br/>pull to chest, kick through"] --> BC
```
