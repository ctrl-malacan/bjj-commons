# The Knee Slice Chain

**Guard break → Knee slice → Knee shield encountered → Response → Side control or mount**

The knee slice is the highest-percentage pass in this reference. Almost everyone defends it with a knee shield, so the chain is really about what you do when the shield goes up — there are five answers, and each one is chosen by how the opponent is defending.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    CG["Closed guard (top)"] -->|"break the guard<br/>wedge, Sao Paulo, or standing"| KS["Knee slice entry<br/>right knee on inner thigh"]
    KS -->|"no shield"| SC["Side control"]
    KS -->|"knee shield goes up"| SH{"Knee shield"}
    SH -->|"shield is low or weak"| PUSH["Push the knee through<br/>slice to side control"]
    SH -->|"shield is high and strong"| RESET["Reset: elevate their knees,<br/>clear the shield, re-slice"]
    SH -->|"they stay low, legs reachable"| LL["Leg lace"] --> SC
    SH -->|"nothing is working"| ST["Stand up, reset to a toreando"]
    SH -->|"they hook your leg"| TOR["Threaten the toreando"]
    TOR -->|"they straighten the leg<br/>to follow"| KS
    SH -->|"same side is stuck"| SW["Switch knees to the other side"]
    SW -->|"opposite-side smash pass"| SC
    SW -->|"knee lace → crossface"| M["Mount"]
    SH -->|"slice stalls, they brace for a head attack"| AL["Arm lock transition<br/>180° pivot"]
    PUSH --> SC
    RESET --> KS
```
