# Technique Chains

One of the most important concepts in jiu-jitsu is that techniques do not exist in isolation. They exist in chains — sequences where one technique creates the opening for the next, and your opponent's defense to one attack feeds directly into another. These are the major chains in this reference so far.

6. [The Mount Submission Chain](06-mount-submission.md)
7. [The Shoulder Clamp Chain](07-shoulder-clamp.md)

9. [The Arm Drag Chain](09-arm-drag.md)
10. [The Carving System Chain](10-carving-system.md)
11. [The Octopus Guard Chain](11-octopus-guard.md)
12. [The Front Headlock System](12-front-headlock.md)

These chains are not scripts to memorize. They are maps of the decision tree. In a live roll, you will rarely execute a full chain from start to finish. What matters is recognizing which branch you are on and knowing what your options are from there.
