# The Carving System Chain

**Side control → Wrap the arm, switch hips, sprawl → North-south → They push → Finish**

Side control is a way station. The carving system leaves it toward north-south with one arm isolated, and every finish is chosen by what the opponent does about that arm.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    SC["Side control<br/>on their right side"] -->|"wrap the arm, switch hips, sprawl"| TR["Moving to north-south<br/>hip in their face"]
    SC -->|"or: arm UNDER their armpit<br/>instead of wrapped"| HID["Hidden setup<br/>both arms on the hips"]
    TR -->|"they push you across —<br/>resist, then go"| NS["North-south, arm isolated"]
    TR -->|"during the big push:<br/>follow their right arm"| HC["Heart choke<br/>other side"]
    TR -->|"arm-out over the shoulder,<br/>shoulder into the neck"| NSC1["North-south choke<br/>(easy to lose)"]
    TR -->|"they clasp their hands"| NSC2["North-south choke<br/>opposite side"]
    NS -->|"the carve"| CV{"They react"}
    CV -->|"nothing yet"| AL["Armlock"]
    CV -->|"arm comes in front<br/>of your head"| KM["Kimura"]
    KM -->|"they link their arms"| GW["Gift wrap → chair sit"] --> BC["Back control"]
    HID -->|"they push you across —<br/>elbow on the neck, they push it"| HAL["Hidden armlock"]
```
