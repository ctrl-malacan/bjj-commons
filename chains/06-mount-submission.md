# The Mount Submission Chain

**Mount → Pressure → They react → Submission or back take**

You don't force a submission on a flat, defensive opponent in mount. You make them uncomfortable enough to move, and then each way they can move has an answer.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    M["Mount<br/>crossface pressure"] --> R{"They react"}
    R -->|"they turn and reach"| GW["Gift wrap"] --> TM["Technical mount"] --> BC["Back control"]
    BC --> AB1["Armbar"]
    BC --> RT["Reverse triangle"]
    R -->|"they hip escape"| HD["Hip drop, ankle staple"] --> M
    R -->|"they shrimp for<br/>knee-elbow escape"| GU["Guillotine"]
    R -->|"they stay flat"| WE["Walk the elbow up"] --> HA["Head-and-arm"] --> SM["S-mount"] --> AB2["Armbar"]
```
