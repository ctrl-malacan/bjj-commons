# The Octopus Guard Chain

**Bottom half guard → Kimura threat → Swim under, sit up, armpit grip → Sweep to top or take the back**

Two sit-up attacks from bottom half guard that share an entry — the kimura threat — and share a rule: reach back for the far armpit or get crossfaced. The butterfly hook version is what happens when they keep you flat.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    HG["Bottom half guard<br/>their right leg trapped"] -->|"fake the kimura, swim<br/>under their left arm"| F{"Can you sit up?"}
    F -->|"yes: left hip to their hip,<br/>left arm under the far armpit"| OCT["Octopus guard"]
    OCT -->|"right leg spreads their knee,<br/>left leg over the back of their leg"| LO["Leg-over flip"] --> HGT["Half guard top<br/>deny their butterfly hook"]
    OCT -->|"they sit back on their hips"| AS["Take their left ankle,<br/>shelve it on your knee"] --> ROLL["Roll them over the hip"] --> PASS["Staple and pass"]
    F -->|"no: they sat to their hip"| BH["Left butterfly hook under their hip"]
    BH -->|"they reach back to clear it"| KT["Grab the arm, kick through"] --> QG["Quarter guard on top"]
    BH -->|"they center over the hook"| EL["Elevate"]
    EL -->|"kick the right leg through,<br/>switch hips, turn them"| BC["Back control"]
    EL -->|"they block the hip switch"| FF["Release, figure-four both legs"]
    FF --> BC
    FF -->|"roll through"| M["Mount"]
    FF -->|"they push you down"| AB["Armbar"]
```
