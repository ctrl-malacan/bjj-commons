# The Shoulder Clamp Chain

**Butterfly / half guard / closed guard → Shoulder clamp → Sweep or arm lock**

One grip — underhook plus a gable grip clamping their shoulder — that you can reach from three guards. Both sweeps land in the same finishing position, so the opponent's defense to the first sweep sets up the second.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    BG["Butterfly guard"] --> CL["Shoulder clamp<br/>underhook + gable grip"]
    HG["Half guard"] --> CL
    CG["Closed guard"] --> CL
    CL -->|"pull into butterfly hooks"| S1["Sweep to your left<br/>kick their leg, hook over"]
    S1 -->|"sweep works"| MA["Mount, their arm splayed"] --> AM["Americana"]
    S1 -->|"they defend"| S2["Clamp their underhook,<br/>sweep to your right"] --> MA
    CL -->|"they shift weight"| AL["Arm lock<br/>shoulder to chin pocket, leg clamp"]
```
