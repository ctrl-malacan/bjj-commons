# The Half Guard Wrestle-Up Chain

**Bottom half guard → Underhook → Wrestle-up → Dogfight → Front headlock system**

The main offensive line from bottom half guard. Win the underhook, get to your knees, and you arrive in the dogfight — where the opponent's choice of defense picks your finish. If they sprawl on you, the chain hands off to the front headlock system.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    HG["Bottom half guard<br/>on your side, their leg trapped"] -->|"use legs to make space"| UH["Win the underhook"]
    UH -->|"arms down, single leg,<br/>technical lift"| DF{"Dogfight<br/>both on all fours"}
    DF -->|"they turn away"| ANK["Grab the far ankle → sweep"]
    DF -->|"they turn toward you"| KNEE["Grip the far knee, drive through"]
    DF -->|"they stay neutral"| BL["Explode up, body lock"] --> BC["Back control"]
    DF -->|"they sprawl"| FH["Front headlock"]
    FH --> AN["Anaconda"]
    FH --> DA["D'Arce"]
    FH --> GU["Guillotine"]
    FH --> BC
```
