# The Arm Drag Chain

**Two-on-one → Break posture → Drag (or clamp) → Back take, sweep, or arm lock**

One grip on one wrist becomes control of the whole body. The first fork is whether the arm will come across their center line at all; every branch after that is chosen by how they try to free it.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    S["Standing"] --> TO["Two-on-one on the wrist"]
    CG["Closed guard"] --> TO
    TO -->|"standing: pull straight down<br/>closed guard: pull in with the legs"| BP["Posture broken,<br/>hand behind the tricep"]
    BP -->|"arm comes across"| DR["Arm drag"]
    DR -->|"sit to the hip, reach the far armpit"| BC["Back control"]
    DR -->|"they are pinned on you"| PS["Pendulum sweep"] --> M["Mount"]
    DR -->|"leg over the shoulder"| AL["Arm lock position"]
    AL -->|"they post and posture"| QAL["Quick arm lock<br/>180° roll"]
    BP -->|"arm will not come across"| CL["Clamp: far armpit,<br/>chest to chest"]
    CL -->|"they post the other arm"| PA["Pinch it into your neck"]
    PA -->|"release legs, kick their knee"| SW["Clamp sweep"]
    SW -->|"they sit back"| BH["Butterfly hook in the pocket"] --> SW
    SW -->|"on top: pull the arm away"| KL["Key lock"]
    KL -->|"they straighten the arm"| SAL["Straight arm lock"]
```
