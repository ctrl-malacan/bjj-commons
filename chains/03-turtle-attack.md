# The Turtle Attack Chain

**Front headlock → Opponent turtles → Choke threat → Knee insertion → Attacks**

When an opponent turtles to escape the front headlock, threatening the choke makes them defend with an arm — and the space that opens is where your knee goes. Everything after the knee insertion is decided by what they do with that space.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    FH["Front headlock"] -->|"they turtle"| T["Turtle (top)<br/>on their side, hip to hip"]
    T -->|"threaten the choke"| DEF{"They defend<br/>with the arm"}
    DEF -->|"space opens between<br/>elbow and knee"| KI["Insert the knee"]
    KI -->|"their arm is trapped"| CR["Crucifix"]
    KI -->|"they reach around your hips"| GU["Guillotine"]
    KI -->|"kick through, get the hook"| BC["Back control"]
    KI -->|"they roll"| POST["Post, recover the seatbelt"] --> BC
```
